 Restaurant
 Röstihüs 
 Long live tradition! Enjoy regional and authentic cuisine in our in-house restaurant Röstihüs.
In our Röstihüs we focus on hospitality and regionality. According to our guests, we serve what are probably the best Röstis and Cordon Bleus in the region. We are happy to serve you a glass of fine Valais wine to accompany your meal or satisfy your thirst with fresh Appenzell beer.
Of course, we also have a small selection of dishes for our youngest guests.
