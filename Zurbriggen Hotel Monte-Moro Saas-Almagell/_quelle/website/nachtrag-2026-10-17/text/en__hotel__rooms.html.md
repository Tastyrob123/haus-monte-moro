 Nice rooms – nice holidays 
 All rooms have shower/WC and are equipped with hair dryer, TV, safe and balcony. For your safety, our hotel is equipped with a fire warning system. 
 Family rooms 
 We have beautiful familyrooms and family-combinations – ideal for 3 - 6 persons.

 We also offer hotel rooms at Residence Alpenhof, a 4-minute walk away (breakfast and dinner at Hotel Monte-Moro).
