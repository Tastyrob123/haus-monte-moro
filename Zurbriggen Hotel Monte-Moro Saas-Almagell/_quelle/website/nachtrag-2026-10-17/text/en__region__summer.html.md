 Activities in Summer 
 You will find a hiking paradise. More than 250 km of hiking trails. Choices as you wish between easy walks, hiking tours and climbing tours to mountains of more than 4000 m. Other sport offers: tennis, biking, scooters, swimming, mini golf
 Are you looking for an extraordinary challenge? We have the right things: Adventure Forest or Alpin Gorge (Cross a mountain river gorge with a mountain guide).
 In the Saas Valley, you will find well developed and safe hiking trails. Our Hotel can be used as starting point of many excursions in a great nature you will never forget. Enjoy the mild climate down in the Valley or the rough climate high in the mountains. Below, we suggest some of our favourite tours.
