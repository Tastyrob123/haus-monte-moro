Daily tours starting in Saas-Almagell to

-

Saas-Fee: Metro Alpin, with the world highest turning restaurant at 3'500 M. Fee-Blitz: the alpin toboggan run

- Stresa (Italy): by the Lago Maggiore, Boat trip to the Boromäic islands and visit of the castles

- Grande Dixence: highest dam of the world
- Sitten/Sion: Provincial capital with visits of the castles Tourbillon and Valeria
